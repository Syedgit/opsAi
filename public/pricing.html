Common questions

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. Sign up, add stores, and use WhatsApp. After 14 days the fee is $25 times the number of locations.

What is an AI reading?

A photo or message we send to the model: a close sheet, an invoice, a schedule, a price board. Short replies like OK or FIX do not count.

Is there a message limit?

Not on the plan. We track readings and estimated AI cost per store so we can see if $25 still covers a busy location. A hidden safety cap still stops a stuck phone from running the bill.

Can I cancel?

Yes. The trial ends on its own if you do nothing. After you add a card you can cancel from Plan and usage before the next month. Cards are processed by Stripe.
