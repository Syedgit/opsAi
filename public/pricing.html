Common questions

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. Sign up, add stores, and use WhatsApp. After 14 days you subscribe at your plan’s per-business rate.

How is billing counted?

Each active business is one seat on the plan. Five Pro businesses is 5 × $45.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Open Billing in the app to manage your card and plan. Features like books look-ups unlock with Pro and above — no separate add-on charge.

Can I cancel?

Yes. The trial ends on its own if you do nothing. After you add a card you can cancel from Plan and usage before the next month. Cards are processed by Stripe.
